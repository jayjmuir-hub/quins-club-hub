# Chat round 4 — the message menu, pins, stars, reply-privately

**Status: NOT SHIPPED — in progress on `claude/chat-round-4`.** Jay's
fourth feedback round, 24 Aug 2026 (late night), relayed from the glass
session with two WhatsApp screenshots and the rulings confirmed with him
there; the pin ruling is his: **anyone in the chat may pin (the WhatsApp
default); staff/admins can always unpin anything.**

## The items

1. **The reaction pill sits WHERE WHATSAPP PUTS IT** — a small tally pill
   overlapping the bubble's bottom corner, hanging just below the edge
   (bottom-left of theirs, bottom-right of yours). Round 3's side trigger
   stays; this moves the TALLIES.
2. **A chevron on the bubble opens the message menu** (replacing round 3's
   inline text actions): Reply · Forward · Copy · Pin/Unpin · Star/Unstar ·
   Reply privately (groups, others' messages) · Delete (yours) / Report
   (theirs). No AI row, obviously.
3. **Copy** — clipboard, client-only.
4. **Pin** — the existing `messages.pinned` column, newly reachable in
   DMs/groups by ANY participant through a new
   `public.set_message_pinned` RPC. ⚠️ NOT by widening the "message edit"
   policy: the UPDATE grant covers (body, pinned, deleted_at) as a set, so
   a participant row-pass would also hand them body-rewrites of other
   people's messages — the exact trap grants.sql §4 documents. The RPC is
   SECURITY DEFINER with a hard-coded single-column UPDATE. Channels keep
   staff-only pinning (announce-only ethos; the ruling was made in the
   group context). Pinned messages render as a banner at the top of the
   thread; tap jumps.
5. **Star** — private bookmarks: `message_stars`, owner-only RLS (the
   nicknames pattern), starred list reachable from the Chats list, only
   ever the owner's.
6. **Reply privately** — from a group message: open (or create) the DM
   with its author and send with the group message QUOTED. The round-2
   quote guard required same-conversation and must relax: the new rule is
   dm-only AND the quoted message readable BY THE SENDER (the guard's
   security-invoker SELECT enforces readability for free). The
   recipient-side leak the old rule prevented is accepted for this
   feature — it is exactly what reply-privately IS, and WhatsApp's
   default. Trigger updated in the migration.

## Out of scope

The dock (stays thin — no menu, inline tallies); channels' pin rules
(staff-only stands); starring from the dock.
