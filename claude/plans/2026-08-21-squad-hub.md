# Squad Hub — the coach/manager dashboard

**Status: shipped** (this PR — see claude/changelog.md).

Jay, 21 Aug 2026: *"create a age group specific coach/manager dashboard, it
would open on a new page like the other dashboards, then move some specific
things into it, also i want to create a system that gives coaches a detailed
view of availability and tracking of attendance."*

## What existed before this, and what did not

Per-event availability (RSVP, `src/screens/Availability.jsx`) and per-event
attendance (the register, `src/screens/Register.jsx`) both existed and were
being written to. What did NOT exist was any aggregate view — no season grid,
no attendance %, no "said yes and didn't show" — and no squad-scoped page for
the people who run one squad. This plan adds the read view; **no schema
changes**, both tables already carry the data.

## Decisions (asked and answered, 21 Aug 2026)

1. **Own page per squad**, not another /admin tab: `/squad` and
   `/squad/:teamId`. Coaches with one squad land straight in it; multi-squad
   staff and admins get a picker.
2. **Everything moves in**: schedule & fixtures, roster, training plans,
   noticeboard, and the new tracking view.
3. **Tracking is a season grid** — players × events, per-player percentages —
   not a per-event summary list.
4. **Availability and attendance sit side by side** per event: what they said
   vs what happened. ⚠️ Shown side by side, never computed one from the
   other — src/lib/features.js carries the warning and it holds here.

## Shape

- **Gate**: `canEditTeam(memberships, teamId)` — squad staff of THAT squad,
  plus admins. The gate is a "not your squad" message, not security: RLS on
  availability/attendance/players already decides what comes back
  (claude/decisions/2026-08-10-role-dashboards.md pattern).
- **"Moving" relocates the coach's front door, not the screens.** Roster,
  Schedule, EventDetail, Notices all stay — parents use them. The hub links
  into them.
- **Sections**: Upcoming (next events with RSVP counts and match-sheet status
  chips — match sheets only for non-minis, reusing `isMinisTeam`), Tracking
  (below), Roster card, Training card, squad NoticeBoard (reused component).
- **Tracking grid**: past events newest-first, players down the side.
  Each cell: RSVP mark (in/maybe/out/no reply) beside attendance mark
  (present/absent/excused/not recorded). Per player: attendance % computed as
  present/(present+absent) — **excused excluded from both sides**, per the
  ruling in src/data/attendance.js `listAttendanceForPlayer`; and a
  "said in, marked absent" count. Filter: All / Training / Matches.
- **Data**: new `listAttendanceForEvents(eventIds)` in src/data/attendance.js,
  chunked with `fetchByIds` exactly like `listAvailabilityForEvents` — the
  400-uuid URL ceiling is measured on this project.
- **Entry points**: staff see a Squad Hub card on the home Dashboard.

## Arguments against, recorded

- *Another dashboard when /admin has portals?* The audience is different:
  AdminDashboard gates on `isAdmin()` before rendering its Outlet, so a coach
  can never see anything nested there — the same reason /notices lives outside
  /admin.
- *Derive attendance from availability (or vice versa) to fill gaps?* Refused
  — they answer different questions with different write authorities, and an
  attendance % that reads RSVP reports who SAID they would come as who came.
