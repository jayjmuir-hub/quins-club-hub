# The Chats list — chat, reshaped like WhatsApp

**Status: SHIPPED 24 Aug 2026** (one pull request; the migration is
`db/migrations/20260824_chat_list.sql`). Supersedes the screen shapes in
`claude/plans/2026-08-23-squad-chat.md`; that plan's database model is
unchanged and still the reference for who may read and post what.

## Why

Jay, 23 Aug 2026, the evening direct messages shipped:

> I don't like the way the messages are setup, especially the DM's, there is
> no logical way to send someone a DM and then when you do those messages
> appear on the main chat area without even showing who they went to, need to
> rethink the chat area and make it more like whatsapp.

Two complaints. The second was a bug — the club channel listed every message
with no squad, and a DM has no squad either — fixed separately the same night
(`79a3d31`). The first was the design: phases 1–3 were built CHANNEL-first
(pick a squad, then a tab, then a "Messages" link for DMs). WhatsApp is
CONVERSATION-first. This plan is that inversion.

Then: "need to be able to delete messages and entire chats too."

## What shipped

1. **One Chats list at `/chat`** (`src/screens/ChatList.jsx`). Squad channels,
   the squad's staff channel (staff only), the club channel and every DM, in
   one list, newest first, with an unread badge and a one-line preview that
   says who spoke. Search. `Chat` in the nav ALWAYS lands here — Jay: "the list
   always". The data is one RPC, `public.my_chats()`, scoped by the same
   helpers the policies use.
2. **The pencil = new chat** (`src/components/NewChatPicker.jsx`). The people
   picker over `dm_candidates()`, grouped by the squad you share, searchable.
   Pick a person, the thread opens. That is the whole DM flow.
3. **A header bar on every thread** (`src/components/ChatHeader.jsx`): back
   to the list, who/what this is, and one line saying exactly who reads it —
   "27 members · announce-only", "Staff only", "Private · you and Sam". The
   ⋯ menu holds the things that happen to a whole chat: announce-only, block,
   delete chat.
4. **Bubbles** (`src/components/MessageRow.jsx`): mine on the right, theirs on
   the left with name and role pill; staff posts keep the red rule; replies
   as an indented thread under the post; actions in a row under the bubble.
5. **Delete a message**: the author, any time (was 15 minutes). Words can
   still only be edited for 15 minutes — that limit moved into the trigger.
6. **Delete a chat** (DMs): cleared FOR YOU, WhatsApp's meaning
   (`conversation_clears`, `clear_conversation()`). The other side keeps
   their copy; if they write again the chat comes back from there. Squad,
   staff and club channels are not deletable — they are the squad.

## What went

The squad Picker, the Squad / Staff-only tabs (the staff channel is its own
row), the separate `/chat/dm` inbox (redirects to the list), the "Messages" and
"Other channels" links. The fixture picker in the composer stayed — a parent
under announce-only still starts a fixture thread with it.

## Unchanged underneath

Every database rule from phases 1–3: who may post, who may DM whom
(`private.can_dm`), adults-only DMs private unless reported
(`claude/decisions/2026-08-23-adult-dms-private-unless-reported.md`), push,
reports, the Welfare dashboard. This was a screen rebuild, not a data one —
which is why it took an evening, not a week.

## Proof

`db/tests/chat-list.sql` — nine assertions against production in a rolled-back
transaction: the list's scope for a parent and a coach, unread counts, a DM
on both sides and invisible to a third, newest-first, author deletes an
hour-old message, cannot edit it, delete-chat semantics on both sides, an
outsider cannot clear. `tests/chat-list.test.jsx`, `tests/chat.test.jsx`,
`tests/direct-messages.test.jsx` for the screens. Harness scenarios
`chat-list` and `message-row` (the latter rendered blank since phase 2 —
no router — fixed here).
